### 1. Module Name

The name of the RTL module is `Piezo_Driver`.

The `Piezo_Driver` module is designed to control a piezoelectric transducer (referred to as "Piezo" in the context of this module). Its primary functions include generating a signal to drive the piezo based on input parameters, acknowledging write operations, and managing the timing of the piezo signal based on a configurable duration. This module plays a crucial role in systems requiring precise control over piezoelectric elements, such as in acoustic applications, precision positioning systems, or haptic feedback mechanisms.

- **Piezo Signal Generation**: The `Piezo` output toggles when the `count` equals the `compare` value, provided the piezo is enabled (as indicated by the most significant bit of the `data` input). This creates a square wave with a period defined by the `data` input, driving the piezo transducer.
